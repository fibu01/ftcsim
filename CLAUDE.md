# ftcsim

Read `docs/PRD.md` first. Phases and acceptance criteria are there.

- Stack: Vite, TypeScript, Three.js, Rapier. Static site, no backend.
- Sim SDK method names come from `reference/ftc-sdk-samples/`, never from memory.
- Field sizes are placeholders tagged [confirm in manual] until the Competition Manual field section is in `docs/`.
- No team number, no student names anywhere.
- Voice in all text: plain words, contractions, no em dashes.
- Game vocabulary (POLLEN, NECTAR, HIVE, CELL, FLOWER) from `docs/game-postcard.txt`.
