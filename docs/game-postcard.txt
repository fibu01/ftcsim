GAMEPLAY
ALLIANCES of two teams each compete to score POLLEN and
NECTAR into their HIVES and FLOWERS. When enough POLLEN or
NECTAR is launched into the CELL the HIVE will TIP. Each time a
HIVE is TIPPED points are earned, and additional NECTAR is
unlocked.

AUTO: During the first 30 seconds of the MATCH, the ROBOTS
operate autonomously. ROBOTS can use sensors to collect
POLLEN from the FLOWERS and navigate to launch into their HIVE.

TELEOP: During the remaining 2 minutes of the MATCH, drivers
take control of their ROBOT and continue to launch into their HIVE.
As time runs out, ALLIANCES work to fill FLOWERS with POLLEN
and NECTAR. ALLIANCES earn points by placing their NECTAR in
each FLOWER before their opponent. ALLIANCES also earn points
for owning a FLOWER if their NECTAR is the top-most placed in a
FLOWER.

The ALLIANCE that earns the most points wins the MATCH and
additional RANKING POINTS can be earned though completing
other scoring achievements.
